Choose a SWF, up to 20 MiB. Files stay in your browser.
Compatibility and isolation

AVM1 and AVM2 support comes from pinned Ruffle 0.7.1. Compatibility is incomplete. Features that require networking, persistent shared objects, external files, or JavaScript integration are unavailable here.

The player has an opaque origin, no storage permissions, no direct network connections, and no browser navigation permissions. Its WebAssembly bytes are delivered from the parent page through a restricted asset broker. Script access and external links are disabled. Complex movies may still consume substantial CPU or memory; close the movie to dispose the player.
